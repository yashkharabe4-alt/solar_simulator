ℹ️ About This Simulation

This simulation models the real-world behavior of a solar panel array, including:

  • Solar Position Tracking: Calculates the sun's altitude and azimuth based on location and time
  • Radiation Calculation: Estimates solar radiation at the Earth's surface accounting for air mass
  • Angle of Incidence: Computes the angle between sunlight and panel surface for accurate power calculation
  • Energy Generation: Uses realistic efficiency curves and temperature coefficients
  • 24-Hour Profile: Shows expected energy generation throughout a full day

Location: Set your latitude and longitude for accurate solar calculations. Default is Pune, India.

Note: This simulation uses a simplified but physically accurate model. Real-world factors like cloud cover, humidity, and dust are not included.
